2013 Strategic Overview in Hong Kong

In 2013, TerraHash Energy established its Asia-Pacific Operational Headquarters in Hong Kong to spearhead sustainable energy solutions across the region. Recognizing Hong Kong's critical transition toward decarbonization, TerraHash Energy deployed cutting-edge solar-wind hybrid systems and specialized circular electricity integration technologies.

Our core mission focused on capturing waste heat and excess output from industrial power generation plants, recycling it into closed-loop renewable grid systems, and helping power generating facilities reduce overall carbon intensity while maximizing efficiency.

2013 Hong Kong Clean Energy Ecosystem

Hong Kong Waste & Energy Blueprint

  • Waste-to-Energy Strategy: Environment Bureau released Hong Kong Blueprint for Sustainable Use of Resources 2013–2022, establishing green energy targets.
  • Buildings Energy Efficiency: Enforced mandatory energy audits to optimize commercial power consumption.

Solar & Wind Infrastructure

  • Lamma Solar Expansion: HK Electric expanded its solar power system to 1 MW capacity at Lamma Power Station.
  • Hydro & Wind Feasibility: Water Supplies Department commissioned a 500kW hydropower plant at Tuen Mun.

Grid Modernization & Energy Shift

  • Scheme of Control Review: HK Government reviewed power utility frameworks to incentivize energy savings and clean grid integration.
  • Fuel Mix Strategy: Initiatives launched to reduce coal dependence (then over 50%) via natural gas and offshore wind.

2013 TerraHash Energy HK Development Timeline

January 2013

Establishment of Asia-Pacific Operational HQ (Central, HK)

TerraHash Energy completed registration in Hong Kong, building a specialized engineering team to deliver utility-scale solar-wind hybrid deployment and power plant energy recycling systems across Greater China.

May 2013

Showcasing Circular Electricity Systems at Eco Expo Asia

TerraHash Energy presented its flagship Circular Power Recycling Framework—designed to divert auxiliary power losses in thermal plants into renewable solar-wind feedback loops—aligning with Hong Kong's new resource blueprint.

Industry Milestone: HK Government officially published the "Hong Kong Blueprint for Sustainable Use of Resources 2013-2022". EPD Hong Kong ↗
August 2013

Launch of Hong Kong Clean Energy R&D Center

Opened a dedicated engineering laboratory focused on microgrid stability, wind-solar energy storage management, and energy efficiency retrofits tailored for large-scale energy infrastructure.

Industry Milestone: HK Science and Technology Parks Corporation (HKSTP) expanded green tech & energy infrastructure R&D. HKSTP Green Tech ↗
November 2013

Green Financing Partnership & Regional Power Projects

Capitalizing on Hong Kong's position as an international financial hub, TerraHash Energy partnered with institutional investors to fund green power transition initiatives and renewable retrofits for utility partners.
