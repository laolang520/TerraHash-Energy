ECOSYSTEM OVERVIEW

Different layers.
Different responsibilities.

Digital currencies operate within an ecosystem of networks, service providers and software. A blockchain records and validates transactions; an exchange facilitates eligible trading; a wallet provides access to keys and on-chain applications; and specialized technology may supply data and market connectivity. These roles should not be confused with energy production, mining operations or each other.

HOW THE ECOSYSTEM WORKS

Four distinct digital-asset roles.

Understanding the function of each layer helps distinguish network infrastructure from financial services, and technology access from asset custody.

01 / BLOCKCHAIN NETWORK

Transactions & settlement records

Bitcoin and other networks use different consensus and validation models. On-chain records do not independently verify off-chain energy measurements or legal rights.

RISK & GOVERNANCE

Know what a platform does—and what it does not.

Digital-asset infrastructure involves separate operational, technical, security and financial risks. The role of each service provider, asset holder and network should be clear before any implementation.

Custody & key control

Identify who controls wallet credentials and who, if anyone, holds customer assets.

Platform & counterparty risk

Review service terms, withdrawal controls, jurisdiction and applicable legal protections.

Network & software risk

Evaluate transaction finality, smart-contract exposure, data sources and security practices.

Commercial & regulatory scope

Any future integrated service requires separate diligence, contracts and applicable approvals.
