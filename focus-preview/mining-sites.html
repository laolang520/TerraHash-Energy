OUR DEVELOPMENT CRITERIA

What makes a site worth evaluating?

01 / POWER

Available Electricity

Potential utility capacity, interconnection requirements, energy supply and reliable service conditions.

02 / COST

Practical Power Economics

Estimated power costs, demand charges and the economics of sustained BTC mining operations.

03 / LAND

Site Suitability

Physical access, usable space, flood exposure, local constraints and opportunity to expand in phases.

04 / APPROVALS

Zoning & Permitting

Permissible use, utility and environmental reviews, noise considerations and other local requirements.

05 / FACILITY

Cooling & Electrical Systems

Potential for mining equipment density, ventilation, heat management, substations and distribution.

06 / PARTNERS

Commercial Execution

Site control, credible suppliers, capital structure, contractor capability and operating responsibilities.

The criteria above describe prospective project evaluation. They do not imply that any specific site, power capacity, permit or mining facility has been secured or commissioned.

FROM LAND TO OPERATIONS

A staged development approach.

Phase 1 — Screen: Identify viable opportunities and assess site, power and local permitting requirements.

Phase 2 — Validate: Review utility terms, project economics, operational assumptions and partner responsibilities.

Phase 3 — Plan: Develop electrical, cooling and mining facility concepts with milestones and risk controls.

Phase 4 — Execute: Move forward only when agreements, financing, approvals and site conditions justify deployment.
