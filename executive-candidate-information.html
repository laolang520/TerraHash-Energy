02 · Corporate Relationship

Hong Kong shareholding. U.S. management autonomy.

What is the Hong Kong relationship?

The Hong Kong TerraHash Energy entity holds an equity interest in TerraHash Energy LLC. The companies use a common TerraHash Energy brand. Their legal entities and responsibilities are distinct.

Who directs U.S. operations?

The Los Angeles company manages its own U.S. strategy, business relationships and day-to-day operating decisions, rather than receiving day-to-day instructions from the Hong Kong shareholder.

Who would employ a U.S. executive?

U.S. roles are presented by TerraHash Energy LLC. The definitive employer, reporting relationships, authority, work location and employment terms are specified in the individual written agreement.

Is the Hong Kong shareholder the U.S. board?

Shareholding, director or manager appointments, and operating authority are different matters. Candidates should rely on the actual U.S. LLC governance and appointment documents rather than assuming a group-level board governs every entity.

03 · Business & Development

What stage is the company at?

Energy and digital infrastructure

TerraHash Energy LLC is building a U.S.-directed infrastructure business focused on energy, storage, site and power strategy, digital infrastructure and computing-related opportunities.

Development versus operating results

Market opportunities, proposed projects and platform concepts are distinct from completed construction, owned facilities, booked revenue or operating capacity. Project-specific commercial and technical details are discussed in the appropriate diligence process.

International historical milestones

Earlier brand, associated-entity and international business milestones are presented as historical context; they do not automatically represent assets or results owned by the California LLC.

Financial and project information

Confidential project pipeline, funding commitments, commercial contracts and financial runway are addressed through authorized executive-level discussions and supporting documentation, where available.

04 · Executive Recruitment

How are executive terms handled?

Scope and decision authority

Each leadership role has a defined mandate. Reporting lines, budget authority, team responsibilities, performance expectations and decision rights are addressed during executive selection and documented in the final appointment or employment agreement.

Base compensation and incentives

Any salary, bonus, equity or long-term incentive arrangement is subject to a written offer and applicable plans or agreements. References to potential equity do not represent an issued award or guaranteed liquidity.

Benefits and work arrangements

Benefits and retirement offerings depend on the programs actually in effect and individual eligibility. Proposed or developing programs should not be read as currently enrolled coverage. Work location and travel expectations are confirmed in the individual offer.

Recruitment communication

Executive inquiries can be directed to hr@terrahashenergy.com. Applicants should review the Applicant Privacy Notice and use company-authorized communications.

This overview supplements, but does not replace, a signed offer, equity plan, employment agreement or governing LLC documents.
