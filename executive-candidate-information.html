02 · Corporate Relationship

Hong Kong shareholding. U.S. management autonomy.

What is the Hong Kong relationship?

The Hong Kong TerraHash Energy shareholder holds equity in TerraHash Energy LLC and has invested capital in the U.S. business. The entities share a brand, but their legal responsibilities and historical financial records remain distinct.

Who directs U.S. operations?

Natalie Hayes, Chief Executive Officer and Executive Director, leads TerraHash Energy LLC's U.S. executive management, business operations, organizational development and operating priorities.

Who would employ a U.S. executive?

U.S. executive roles are offered by TerraHash Energy LLC, a California limited liability company. The company-approved compensation and benefits program is presented in Careers; individual reporting lines, authority, location and employment terms are confirmed in the written offer.

Is the Hong Kong shareholder the U.S. board?

Shareholding, board appointments and day-to-day executive management are distinct responsibilities. The Leadership page introduces people across these functions; a person's listing there does not by itself establish authority over the U.S. LLC.

04 · U.S. Operating Model

U.S. management, capital and project execution

Operating authority

Natalie Hayes leads executive management and day-to-day operations for the Los Angeles company, including U.S. business priorities and project-development oversight. The Hong Kong shareholder provides capital investment but does not manage routine U.S. business operations.

Capital and spending

The Hong Kong shareholder has invested capital in TerraHash Energy LLC. International historical capital programs are presented on Corporate Performance and should not be interpreted as the current U.S. LLC cash balance. Role-level budgets and delegated authority are discussed with qualified executive candidates.

Project-stage clarity

Each opportunity is discussed by project location, customer or partner relationship, contracting entity, development phase, capital responsibility and next execution milestone.

Executive financial review

The hiring team coordinates confidential discussions, as appropriate, of the U.S. project's stage, commercial approach, available operating resources, budgets, customer arrangements and development timelines with qualified executive candidates. Public international history should not be interpreted as a statement of the California LLC's cash balance or guaranteed financial support.

05 · Executive Employment

Terms and leadership responsibilities

Employer and reporting

TerraHash Energy LLC recruits for distinct executive responsibilities in operations, infrastructure development and commercial growth. Reporting relationships, delegated authority, work location and performance expectations are confirmed through the applicable hiring and appointment process.

Salary and long-term incentives

Applicants may request the role pay scale from hr@terrahashenergy.com. Executive salary terms have been approved. Candidates can request the applicable pay scale from hr@terrahashenergy.com; job postings include salary ranges when required by law. Any bonus, RSUs or other equity arrangements are specified in a written offer and the applicable plan, including valuation assumptions, vesting, dilution and liquidity provisions.

Benefits and work arrangements

TerraHash Energy LLC has approved executive compensation and benefits including medical, dental and vision coverage and 401(k) retirement benefits for eligible employees. Specific coverage, plan effective dates, participation requirements, paid leave, work arrangements and any employer contributions follow the applicable offer and plan documents.

Candidate communication

The recruitment team is the primary channel for coordinating candidate diligence, hiring documentation and executive interviews. Contact hr@terrahashenergy.com.
