02 · Corporate Relationship

Hong Kong shareholding. U.S. management autonomy.

What is the Hong Kong relationship?

The Hong Kong TerraHash Energy entity holds an equity interest in TerraHash Energy LLC. The companies use a common TerraHash Energy brand. Their legal entities and responsibilities are distinct.

Who directs U.S. operations?

The Los Angeles company manages its own U.S. strategy, business relationships and day-to-day operating decisions, rather than receiving day-to-day instructions from the Hong Kong shareholder.

Who would employ a U.S. executive?

U.S. roles are presented by TerraHash Energy LLC. The definitive employer, reporting relationships, authority, work location and employment terms are specified in the individual written agreement.

Is the Hong Kong shareholder the U.S. board?

Shareholding, director or manager appointments, and operating authority are different matters. Candidates should rely on the actual U.S. LLC governance and appointment documents rather than assuming a group-level board governs every entity.

04 · U.S. Operating Model

U.S. management, capital and project execution

Operating authority

The Los Angeles company manages its own U.S. executive team, commercial priorities, relationships, contracts and project-development decisions. The Hong Kong company has an equity interest without directing daily operations.

Capital and spending

International funding programs are identified separately from U.S. operating resources. Executive diligence covers the applicable capital arrangement, U.S. budget, spending authority and cash-flow plan.

Project-stage clarity

U.S. development work is ongoing. Each opportunity is discussed by project location, customer or partner relationship, contracting entity, current development phase, capital responsibility and next execution milestone. Projects still being developed are distinguished from completed or operating milestones.

Executive financial review

The hiring team coordinates confidential discussions of project pipeline, revenue model, committed resources, operating budget, current customer arrangements and development timelines with qualified executive candidates.

05 · Executive Employment

Terms and leadership responsibilities

Employer and reporting

U.S. executive positions are presented by TerraHash Energy LLC. The offer and relevant governance documents state reporting lines, delegation, budget authority, work location and performance expectations.

Salary and long-term incentives

Applicants may request the role pay scale from hr@terrahashenergy.com. Salary, bonus, RSUs or other equity arrangements are set out in a written offer and applicable plan, including valuation assumptions, vesting, dilution and liquidity provisions.

Benefits and work arrangements

The written offer identifies active benefits, eligibility, any planned programs, paid leave, retirement options, work model and required travel. Proposed programs are distinguished from benefits currently provided.

Candidate communication

The recruitment team is the primary channel for coordinating candidate diligence, hiring documentation and executive interviews. Contact hr@terrahashenergy.com.
