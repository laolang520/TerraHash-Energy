01 / RENEWABLE ENERGY GENERATION

What is renewable power?

Renewable energy is electricity produced from naturally replenishing resources such as sunlight, wind and water. Solar photovoltaic systems convert sunlight directly into electricity, wind turbines convert moving air into electrical power, and hydroelectric systems use flowing water. These resources can be combined with grid interconnection, battery storage and control systems to provide a more flexible energy platform.

For TerraHash, renewable generation is the physical starting point. Large-scale digital infrastructure requires dependable, cost-aware power, so site selection, generation mix, storage, interconnection and load management are all part of the infrastructure strategy.

  • Solar PV, wind and other renewable generation
  • Battery storage and load balancing
  • Grid interconnection and power distribution
  • Energy monitoring and flexible load management
02 / BITCOIN MINING MACHINES

What is a Bitcoin mining machine?

A Bitcoin mining machine is specialized computing hardware, commonly called an ASIC miner, designed to perform the SHA-256 calculations used by the Bitcoin network. Thousands of these machines can operate together in a mining facility, consuming electrical power and producing hashrate — the computing capacity used to participate in Bitcoin’s proof-of-work process.

Mining is not only about the machine itself. A professional mining operation depends on transformers, switchgear, power distribution, networking, cooling, airflow or liquid-cooling systems, firmware, maintenance, monitoring and uptime management. The economic performance of a miner is closely tied to electricity cost, machine efficiency, network conditions and operational reliability.

  • ASIC hardware converts electricity into hashrate
  • Hashrate contributes computing work to the Bitcoin network
  • Cooling and electrical systems determine operating stability
  • Maintenance, firmware and monitoring protect fleet performance

03 / PERSONAL DIGITAL WALLET

What is a personal digital wallet?

A personal digital wallet is software that allows a user to interact with blockchain-based assets and networks. Wallets such as Trust Wallet are examples of consumer-facing digital wallets. In a self-custody model, the wallet helps the user manage cryptographic keys, authorize transactions, view balances and connect with decentralized applications without requiring a traditional bank account to control the assets.

A wallet does not physically “store” coins inside the phone. The digital assets remain recorded on the blockchain; the wallet manages the credentials and transaction-signing tools that allow the user to access them. Security therefore depends heavily on protecting private keys, seed phrases and device access.

  • Access Bitcoin and supported digital assets
  • Sign and authorize blockchain transactions
  • Connect with decentralized applications and services
  • Self-custody keeps key control with the user

04 / ON-CHAIN EXCHANGE

What is an on-chain exchange?

An on-chain exchange is a digital asset marketplace where trading activity is executed through blockchain networks and smart contracts rather than being recorded only inside a centralized company database. Users typically connect a compatible wallet, approve a transaction and interact directly with blockchain-based liquidity pools, order systems or other decentralized market infrastructure.

This model can provide transparent transaction settlement and direct wallet-to-market connectivity, but it also introduces blockchain-specific considerations such as smart-contract risk, network fees, liquidity depth, transaction finality and regulatory obligations. Within the TerraHash ecosystem, NEXUS is positioned as the downstream on-chain market and liquidity layer.

  • Wallet-to-market connectivity
  • Blockchain-based transaction settlement
  • Liquidity access and digital asset exchange
  • Future decentralized market and ecosystem applications
