Chief Operating Officer

LOS ANGELES, CALIFORNIAEXECUTIVE LEADERSHIPOPERATIONS & INFRASTRUCTUREEARLY-STAGE BUILD
WORK MODELLos Angeles preferred · Hybrid / Flexible
TRAVELProject & partner travel expected
FLEXIBILITYFlexible based on project and business needs

The Chief Operating Officer will build the operating foundation behind TerraHash Energy’s growth — translating strategy into disciplined execution across project delivery, internal operations, partner coordination, reporting and organizational scale.

Role Mandate

  • Build the operating model required to scale TerraHash Energy from an early-stage platform into a disciplined infrastructure company.
  • Translate company strategy into clear priorities, operating cadence, accountability and execution plans.
  • Create cross-functional systems that connect development, finance, engineering, partnerships and leadership decision-making.

Key Responsibilities

  • Lead company-wide operating planning, execution tracking and management cadence.
  • Establish KPIs, reporting standards, decision processes and operational controls.
  • Coordinate project execution across development, engineering, procurement, finance and external partners.
  • Build scalable internal processes for budgeting, vendor management, contracting, risk management and organizational operations.
  • Support leadership on capital deployment, resource allocation and strategic priorities.
  • Help recruit, structure and develop the operating team as the company grows.

Preferred Profile

  • 10+ years of operating, infrastructure, energy, industrial or project execution experience.
  • Experience building or scaling operations in a growth-stage, project-driven or asset-heavy business.
  • Strong financial and operational judgment with the ability to manage multiple workstreams and counterparties.
  • Comfort working across strategy and detail — from executive planning to implementation discipline.
  • Builder mindset with high ownership, strong communication and a bias toward execution.

What You Will Shape

  • TerraHash Energy’s operating model, management cadence and internal execution standards.
  • Cross-functional coordination between development, engineering, finance and strategic partners.
  • Organizational systems and leadership processes required for long-term scale.
  • Operational readiness across the company’s growing infrastructure platform.

Compensation & Benefits
