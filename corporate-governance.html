Public Governance Overview

Governance & leadership responsibilities

This page summarizes public governance principles and management roles. Internal corporate policies, appointment records, approval procedures and employment documents are not distributed through this website.

01

U.S. Executive Management

TerraHash Energy LLC's U.S. business is led by Natalie Hayes, Chief Executive Officer and Executive Director, who directs operating priorities, organizational leadership and U.S. infrastructure development activities.

02

Corporate Governance & Accountability

Board, committee and management roles follow the applicable appointments of their respective corporate entities. Governance oversight is distinct from day-to-day U.S. operating management.

03

Financial Controls & Risk Management

Project evaluation, spending decisions and contracting are conducted within the company's authorized management and control processes, with attention to operating, financial and compliance risks.

04

Responsible Business Practices

TerraHash Energy LLC emphasizes professional conduct, documented business decisions, responsible recruitment and clear communication with employees and counterparties.
