Official communications

Official TerraHash Energy email communications use the @terrahashenergy.com domain. Visitors should independently verify unexpected requests, payment instructions or identity claims by contacting the company through the official website or corporate email address shown above.

Information collected through this website

When you voluntarily submit an inquiry, TerraHash Energy may receive information such as your name, email address, company or organization, professional role, project location, inquiry category and the message or supporting information you choose to provide.

How information is used

Information submitted through the website is used to review and respond to inquiries, route communications to the appropriate business function, evaluate potential projects or partnerships, maintain business correspondence and protect the website and company from misuse.

Service providers and transmission

Website inquiry submissions may be transmitted through third-party infrastructure used to operate the contact form and corporate email service. Do not submit passwords, private keys, wallet recovery phrases, banking credentials, government identification numbers or other highly sensitive credentials through the public website form.

Supporting documents

Where the website permits supporting documents, submit only materials that you are authorized to share. Files may be transmitted with the associated inquiry and should not contain sensitive credentials or unnecessary confidential personal information.

Data retention and choices

Business correspondence may be retained for a reasonable period for operational, legal, security and recordkeeping purposes. To request correction or deletion of information you submitted through this website, contact ir@terrahashenergy.com. Requests may be subject to legal or recordkeeping obligations.

Corporate verification & anti-impersonation

Verification notice. TerraHash Energy will not ask website visitors to disclose passwords, private keys or wallet recovery phrases. Be cautious of individuals using look-alike domains, personal email addresses or unofficial messaging accounts while claiming to represent TerraHash Energy. Verify material communications through terrahashenergy.com and ir@terrahashenergy.com.

Policy updates

This notice may be updated as the website, business processes or service providers evolve. Last updated: September 2026.
