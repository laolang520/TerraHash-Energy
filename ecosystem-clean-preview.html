WHAT WE DO

Energy-to-digital infrastructure.

TerraHash is positioned around one clear pathway: secure and optimize electricity, convert that electricity into productive Bitcoin network compute, and connect digital assets with user-facing wallet and on-chain market infrastructure.

CORE BUSINESS

Three operating layers.

02 / BITCOIN COMPUTE

BITCOIN MINING INFRASTRUCTURE

Mining is a power and operations system.

An ASIC miner is specialized hardware designed to perform SHA-256 calculations for the Bitcoin network. But a professional mining operation is not defined by the machine alone. Site power, transformers, switchgear, cooling, firmware, maintenance, networking and uptime management all determine how effectively electricity is converted into hashrate.

01
Power Systems

Electrical architecture designed around continuous, high-density computing loads.

02
ASIC Fleet

Machine deployment, firmware, health monitoring, repair and lifecycle management.

03
Thermal & Site Operations

Cooling, airflow, networking and physical infrastructure that protect uptime.

04
Hashrate Intelligence

Fleet-level visibility into output, efficiency and operating performance.
