01 / RENEWABLE ENERGY

What is renewable electricity?

Renewable electricity is power generated from naturally replenishing resources such as sunlight, wind and flowing water. Solar PV modules convert sunlight directly into electricity; wind turbines convert wind into electrical power; hydro systems use moving water. These sources can be combined with battery storage, substations, grid interconnection and control systems.

For TerraHash, renewable generation is the physical foundation. Digital infrastructure requires dependable and cost-aware electricity, so generation mix, storage, grid access and flexible load management are part of the same infrastructure decision.

  • Solar, wind and other renewable generation
  • Battery storage and load balancing
  • Substations, transformers and grid interconnection
  • Power monitoring and flexible-load strategy
02 / BITCOIN MINING

What is a Bitcoin mining machine?

A Bitcoin mining machine is specialized computing hardware, commonly known as an ASIC miner, built to perform SHA-256 calculations for the Bitcoin network. When many miners operate together they create hashrate — the computing power used to participate in Bitcoin’s proof-of-work process.

A professional mining site is much more than machines. It includes electrical distribution, switchgear, transformers, networking, cooling, firmware, repair, machine-health monitoring and uptime management. Electricity cost, hardware efficiency and operational reliability strongly influence mining economics.

  • ASIC hardware converts electricity into hashrate
  • Hashrate performs proof-of-work computation
  • Cooling and power systems support stable operation
  • Maintenance and monitoring protect fleet performance
03 / PERSONAL DIGITAL WALLET

What is a personal digital wallet?

A personal digital wallet is software that lets a user interact with blockchain assets and networks. Consumer wallets such as Trust Wallet illustrate the self-custody model: users can manage credentials, authorize transactions, view assets and connect to decentralized applications.

The wallet does not literally store coins inside the phone. Assets are recorded on the blockchain; the wallet manages the cryptographic keys and transaction-signing tools that allow a user to control and access them. Protecting private keys and recovery phrases is therefore essential.

  • Access Bitcoin and supported digital assets
  • Authorize and sign blockchain transactions
  • Connect with decentralized applications
  • Self-custody keeps key control with the user

04 / ON-CHAIN EXCHANGE

What is an on-chain exchange?

An on-chain exchange is a digital asset market where transactions are executed through blockchain networks and smart contracts rather than recorded only in a centralized internal database. A user generally connects a compatible wallet, approves the transaction and interacts with blockchain-based liquidity or market infrastructure.

Within TerraHash’s ecosystem concept, NEXUS represents this downstream market layer — connecting compatible wallets and digital assets with on-chain liquidity, exchange functionality and future blockchain applications.

  • Wallet-to-market connectivity
  • Blockchain-based transaction settlement
  • Liquidity and digital asset exchange
  • Future decentralized applications and services
