Natalie Hayes

Chief Executive Officer · Executive Director

Natalie Hayes is an experienced executive leader with more than two decades of professional experience across corporate operations, renewable energy and strategic business development. She began her career with Xinyi Glass Group in 1999, where she developed broad expertise across finance, sales, manufacturing and operations before advancing into senior management roles.

As Chief Executive Officer of TerraHash Energy, Ms. Hayes has played a key role in overseeing business operations, strategic growth and the development of the Company’s solar energy activities. She continues to contribute her executive expertise as an Executive Director, with a focus on corporate strategy, investment opportunities and long-term business development.
