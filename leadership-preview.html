BOARD OF DIRECTORS

Board of Directors

The Board provides oversight and strategic guidance to the Company, bringing together experience across infrastructure, finance, technology, corporate governance and business development.

BOARD LEADERSHIP

Gu Cheng’an (Cheng’an Gu)

Executive Director & Chairman of the Board

Gu Cheng’an has more than 20 years of experience in the construction industry and holds a Bachelor of Science degree, a Bachelor’s degree in Architectural Engineering and a Master of Science degree in Engineering Management following studies in New Zealand and Hong Kong. He joined Wang Lee Engineering Limited in 2000 and currently serves as its Chief Executive Officer and Chairman. His areas of specialization include building services installation, energy management, energy conservation planning and design. Over more than two decades with Wang Lee Engineering Limited, he has participated in and led the company through multiple stages of development, building extensive leadership and management experience and developing a professional team across technical and operational functions.

PROFILE FOCUS · Building services · Energy management · Energy conservation planning · Engineering management · 20+ years industry experience
INDEPENDENT DIRECTOR

Lu Jingchuan (Jingchuan Lu)

Independent Director & Chair of Compensation Committee

Lu Jingchuan is a director of OJ Studio Pty Ltd with experience spanning design-and-build projects, electrical networks, fiber-optic cabling services, solar installations, energy efficiency and cryptocurrency infrastructure and systems. A registered electrician since 2008, he has worked as a designated contractor on major commercial landmark design-and-build projects, including the Queen Victoria Building and Westfield Sydney, as well as jewelry exhibitions and art-gallery projects in Hong Kong, Paris, London and Sydney. He has also worked as a contractor and certified installer for Ecopense Pty Ltd on residential and commercial solar installations in Australia and Spain. His technical work has included the design and development of computing systems supporting the Ethereum network and integrated solar and cryptocurrency mining equipment designed to operate with energy-efficient solar-array systems. He currently resides in Australia.

PROFILE FOCUS · Electrical systems · Solar installations · Energy efficiency · Fiber networks · Digital-asset infrastructure
INDEPENDENT DIRECTOR

Lin Zhiyuan (Zhiyuan Lin)

Independent Director & Chair of Corporate Governance and Nomination Committee

Lin Zhiyuan graduated in France in 2010 with a Bachelor’s degree in Architecture and Interior Design. His professional experience includes work with architectural practices including Tarlet Architects, Gerzina Architecture, MAKE Creative and Innova Design across commercial, residential, retail and healthcare projects. INDEPENDENT DIRECTOR

Michael Gu (Mingyuan Gu)

Independent Director

Michael Gu has served as an independent director of Troops, Inc., a Nasdaq-listed company, since June 26, 2018. His earlier professional experience includes serving as a senior auditor at Moore Stephens CPA Limited from April 2004 to April 2007 and as a senior accountant at Grant Thornton Hong Kong Limited from November 2007 to February 2008. Subsequent finance-management positions included Global Beverages Asia Limited, Skyworth Digital Holdings Limited and TAL Apparel Limited. From September 2013 to April 2018, he served as Finance Director for property and facilities management services at Synergis Management Services Limited, a subsidiary of Hong Kong-listed Synergis Holdings Limited. He received a Bachelor of Arts degree in Accountancy from The Hong Kong Polytechnic University in 2002 and has been an ACCA-qualified accountant since 2008.

PROFILE FOCUS · Public-company governance · Audit · Accounting · Financial management · ACCA
