BITCOIN & COMPUTE

Infrastructure designed around power, not speculation.

Bitcoin mining is presented as a high-density, energy-sensitive compute application. The infrastructure thesis centers on power availability, hardware efficiency, thermal management, uptime and operational discipline.

01
