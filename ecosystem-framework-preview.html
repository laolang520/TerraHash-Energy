DIGITAL ECOSYSTEM

Wallet access and strategic on-chain connectivity.

The digital layer should stay clearly downstream from the energy and compute platform. This keeps TerraHash institutionally credible while still showing a path into user-facing digital asset applications.

WALLET LAYER

Self-Custody Wallet Connectivity

A personal digital wallet layer can provide users with access to Bitcoin and supported ecosystem assets, while maintaining a clear separation between TerraHash infrastructure and third-party wallet brands.

If Trust Wallet is referenced publicly, the site should describe compatibility or integration only unless a formal relationship has been established.
◎
STRATEGIC ECOSYSTEM PARTNER

Chain Nexus

Chain Nexus can be positioned as a strategic on-chain exchange and liquidity partner connecting TerraHash’s digital asset ecosystem with trading, settlement and broader market access.

Recommended positioning: strategic ecosystem partner rather than a TerraHash-owned exchange, unless the corporate relationship is formally structured that way.
