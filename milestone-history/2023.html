2023 Strategic Overview: Next-Gen Clean Energy & Battery Storage Synergy

In 2023, TerraHash Energy achieved a major technological breakthrough by combining renewable generation with advanced Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS). In a milestone deal, TerraHash partnered with CATL (Contemporary Amperex Technology Co., Limited) to deploy 200MWh+ liquid-cooled lithium battery systems across wind and solar mining sites. This hybrid "Wind/Solar + CATL BESS + Dynamic Compute" model drastically improved renewable energy economics and grid stability worldwide.

2023 Renewable Innovations & CATL Battery Storage Blueprint

CATL BESS Alliance & Smart Microgrids

  • CATL EnerOne Liquid Cooling: Integration of 200MWh+ CATL LFP battery storage modules at major farm hubs.
  • Millisecond Power Response: Ultra-fast power smoothing and grid stabilization during wind/solar fluctuations.

Wind & Solar Economic Enhancement

  • Curtailment Monetization: Converting 95%+ of off-peak wind and midday solar curtailment into high-value hash.
  • Enhanced PPA Economics: Power Purchase Agreements yielding effective power costs under $0.028/kWh.

Hong Kong HK$1B Green Energy Fund

  • HK$1B APAC Energy & Compute Fund: Co-established with CICC and HKSTP for zero-carbon energy tech.
  • Thermal Recycling Pilots: District heating reuse trial transforming ASIC heat into urban hot water networks.

2023 CATL Lithium-Ion Energy Storage Collaboration

The archived 2023 review describes an energy-storage collaboration with CATL, including an initial 200 MWh procurement of EnerOne/EnerC containerized, liquid-cooled lithium iron phosphate (LFP) battery systems.


The microgrid concept integrates batteries with wind- and solar-powered computing sites to manage generation variability, respond rapidly to grid conditions and provide a combination of computing load flexibility and grid-frequency support.

2023 Improved Wind and Solar Economics

The archive reports that combining storage with flexible computing loads reduced electricity curtailment at partner wind and solar installations from 18% to below 2%.


It also reports a blended effective electricity cost below $0.028 per kWh through storage-based peak/off-peak optimization and participation in grid ancillary services.

2023 Hong Kong Green-Energy Fund and Waste-Heat Recovery

The archived report describes a HK$1 billion green-energy storage and computing fund initiated in Hong Kong with CICC and Hong Kong Science and Technology Parks, focusing on battery storage and low-carbon computing infrastructure.


Its pilot projects also explore recovering heat from liquid-cooled ASIC equipment and routing it to greenhouses or district hot-water systems to improve overall energy utilization.

2023 Global Green Computing Capacity Reaches 4.5 EH/s

The archived year-end review reports managed green computing capacity of 4.5 EH/s (4,500 PH/s) by the fourth quarter, following storage deployment across Texas, Northern Europe and Asia-Pacific.


The company described this as a shift from conventional electricity purchasing for mining toward integrated renewable microgrid infrastructure and flexible computing operations.
