2020 Strategic Overview: Industrial Scaling & Renewable Transition

In May 2020, the 3rd Bitcoin Halving reduced block rewards to 6.25 BTC. To maintain competitive profit margins, TerraHash Energy spearheaded the development of 100MW+ Industrial-Scale Mega Mining Farms. By pairing next-gen S19/M30S ASIC fleets directly with Hydroelectric and Wind Power curtailment, TerraHash achieved ultra-low power costs and established a sustainable green computing footprint.

2020 Mega-Farm & Clean Energy Blueprint

100MW+ Mega-Farm Infrastructure

  • Modular Containers: Rapid deployment of high-density, air-cooled and immersion mining pods.
  • Post-Halving Resiliency: Industrial power purchase agreements (PPAs) securing under $0.035/kWh power rates.

Hydro & Wind Power Monetization

  • Hydro Seasonal Balancing: Absorbing wet-season surplus hydropower in South Asia/Nordics.
  • Wind Farm Off-Take: Monetizing grid curtailment and stranded energy from large-scale wind parks.

Flagship ASIC Procurement (110 TH/s)

  • S19 Pro & M30S+ Mass Orders: Fleet transition to 29.5 J/TH ultra-high efficiency hardware.
  • Hashrate Expansion: Global managed capacity exceeded 500 PH/s post-halving.

2020 Bitcoin Halving and 100 MW+ Mining Facilities

On May 11, 2020, Bitcoin's third halving cut block rewards from 12.5 BTC to 6.25 BTC, increasing the importance of power efficiency and economies of scale.


TerraHash's archived expansion plan describes standardized container mining pods connected to high-voltage substations, with target power-purchase agreement prices below $0.035 per kWh.

2020 Hydropower, Wind Curtailment and Green Computing

Seasonal hydropower surpluses offered lower-cost energy, with the archive citing wet-season rates as low as $0.025 per kWh for computing sites located near generation facilities.


Wind farms also experience periods of curtailed output. The project describes using interruptible mining loads to absorb electricity that otherwise could not be delivered to the grid.

2020 ASIC Fleet Upgrade: S19 Pro and M30S

New equipment included the Antminer S19 Pro, rated at approximately 110 TH/s and 29.5 J/TH, and WhatsMiner M30S/M31S series machines with materially improved efficiency.


The archived fleet plan describes a large-scale pre-order program and an intended improvement of more than 50% in average equipment energy efficiency to strengthen resilience after the halving.

2020 Managed Hashrate Exceeds 500 PH/s

The archived year-end report states that commissioning large-scale low-carbon facilities and installing S19-series hardware brought managed computing capacity above 500 PH/s in 2020.


With Bitcoin rising above $20,000 in the fourth quarter, the project positioned its hydro- and wind-powered facilities to participate in improving industry conditions.
