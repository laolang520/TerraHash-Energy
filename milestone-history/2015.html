2015 Milestone Review: Climate Action Alignment, Smart Grid Analytics & Large-Scale Renewable Integration in Hong Kong

2015 Strategic Overview in Hong Kong

In 2015, TerraHash Energy aligned its technology roadmap with the HKSAR Government’s official Hong Kong Climate Change Report 2015. Expanding beyond earlier energy storage pilots, TerraHash pioneered enterprise-level energy analytics, dynamic grid balancing, and automated demand-response solutions across Hong Kong's utility networks and infrastructure projects.

2015 Hong Kong Regional Development Blueprint

Hong Kong Island Grid & Decarbonization

  • Climate Policy 2015: HKSAR targets 50-60% carbon intensity reduction by 2020.
  • HK Electric Smart Grid: Upgraded south Hong Kong Island network with predictive load telemetry.

Tuen Mun & New Territories Gas Hub

  • Black Point D1 CCGT: Approved environmental permits for natural gas turbine expansion.
  • Telemetry Analytics: Microsecond-level grid monitoring for thermal transition.

Tseung Kwan O Water-Energy Infrastructure

  • TKO Desalination Plant: Energy-efficient reverse osmosis auxiliary microgrid design.
  • Floating Solar Expansion: Reservoirs equipped with PV systems to save evaporation loss.

2015 Hong Kong Island Grid and Climate Policy

In November 2015, the Hong Kong government published its Climate Change Report 2015, prioritizing electricity decarbonization and building efficiency, with a target to cut carbon intensity by 50–60% against 2005 levels by 2020.


HK Electric advanced smart-grid telemetry around Lamma Power Station and the southern Hong Kong Island network. The archived project describes TerraHash's clean-energy forecasting and dynamic balancing work to improve reliability during peak demand.

×

2015 Tuen Mun / Black Point Energy Hub

In 2015, the Environmental Protection Department approved the environmental impact assessment permit for Black Point Power Station's D1 combined-cycle gas turbine (CCGT) unit.


To support Hong Kong's transition from coal toward natural gas, TerraHash's project narrative describes microsecond-resolution telemetry intended to balance turbine ramp-up, ramp-down and sudden grid-frequency fluctuations.

×

2015 Tseung Kwan O Water and Energy Infrastructure

The Civil Engineering and Development Department and Water Supplies Department advanced planning for the Tseung Kwan O Area 137 desalination project in 2015, including its clean-energy options.


The project concept paired distributed solar generation with battery storage for auxiliary microgrid power, drawing on lessons from floating solar demonstrations at Shek Pik Reservoir.

×

2015 Central and Admiralty Low-Carbon Buildings

The government's 2015 climate report emphasized improved energy efficiency in commercial buildings, including partnership initiatives in Hong Kong's central business district.


The archived TerraHash plan describes real-time chiller energy monitoring and AI-based alerts for offices in Central and Admiralty, with adaptive off-peak cooling schedules and a reported 8% annual reduction in shared-area energy use.
