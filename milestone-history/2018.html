2018 Strategic Review: HK Feed-in Tariff Launch, Green Bitcoin Mining Expansion & Smart Energy Management

2018 Strategic Overview in Hong Kong

2018 marked a major turning point for TerraHash Energy. Following the official launch of Hong Kong's Feed-in Tariff (FiT) Scheme, TerraHash officially entered the Bitcoin Mining Industry. By integrating distributed renewable solar installations with high-density ASIC mining rigs, TerraHash established eco-friendly computing infrastructure and smart power dispatching systems across Hong Kong.

2018 Regional Development & Mining Blueprint

Hong Kong Feed-in Tariff (FiT) Scheme

  • HK$3 - HK$5 Subsidies: Power companies started buying back solar power at attractive rates.
  • NT Village House Policy: Relaxation of rooftop solar height limits to 2.5m in October 2018.

Entry into Bitcoin Mining Industry

  • ASIC Hardware Fleet: Deployment of Antminer S9/Next-Gen ASIC miners in HK hubs.
  • Green Mining Strategy: Offsetting hash computation energy with local solar generation.

Immersion Cooling & Heat Recovery

  • Liquid Immersion Pilot: High-efficiency liquid cooling for high-density mining rigs.
  • Waste Heat Recycling: Capturing mining thermal energy for industrial reuse.

2018 Hong Kong Feed-in Tariff Rollout

CLP Power began accepting feed-in tariff applications in May 2018, with payments of HK$3–5 per kilowatt-hour for eligible renewable electricity starting in October.


Regulations for rooftop solar installations on New Territories village houses were also relaxed. TerraHash's archive describes helping distributed rooftop projects connect solar generation to the grid.

×

2018 Expansion into Bitcoin Mining

In 2018, TerraHash established a computing and mining division and deployed efficient ASIC equipment, including Antminer S9-series units, drawing on Hong Kong's digital-asset ecosystem.


Its proposed green-computing model combined daytime rooftop solar consumption, the sale of surplus generation to the grid, and off-peak nighttime electricity to help manage the effects of the 2018 crypto bear market.

×

2018 Immersion Cooling and Waste Heat Recovery

Hong Kong's subtropical climate created high cooling demands for dense computing equipment. In the second half of 2018, TerraHash's archive describes trials of single-phase immersion cooling.


Recovered heat was redirected through heat exchangers for greenhouse agriculture and industrial drying, with the project claiming a power usage effectiveness (PUE) below 1.05.

×

2018 Combined Solar and Liquid-Cooled Computing System

A green-computing node in Yuen Long reportedly started operating in October 2018, with a 200 kW rooftop solar array and modular immersion-cooled mining racks.


The installation was presented as an early demonstration of using distributed renewable electricity to directly support blockchain computing and lower the carbon intensity of operations.
