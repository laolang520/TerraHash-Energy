2019 Strategic Review: Bitcoin Market Recovery, Mining Profitability Surges & Next-Gen ASIC Efficiency Deployment

2019 Strategic Overview in Hong Kong & Global Mining

In 2019, the Bitcoin network experienced a dramatic recovery, with BTC prices surging from ~$3,500 to over $13,000 in mid-year. TerraHash Energy capitalized on this profitability boom by deploying next-generation 7nm ASIC hardware, optimizing electricity costs through Hong Kong's Feed-in Tariff arbitrage, and achieving record-breaking profit margins on green computing operations.

2019 Mining Profitability & Fleet Blueprint

  • Efficiency Breakthrough: Upgraded fleet to Antminer S17/T17 and Whatsminer M20S (40–45 J/TH).

FiT Tariff Arbitrage & Power Cost Control

2019 Bitcoin Recovery and Mining Profitability

Bitcoin recovered from its 2018 decline, climbing from approximately $3,400 early in 2019 to above $13,000 in June and July. The recovery materially improved mining margins.


The archived model estimates electricity at $0.04–0.05 per kWh and a shutdown or breakeven BTC price of roughly $3,500–4,500. At the mid-year peak, gross mining margins were estimated at 65–70%.

×

2019 Next-Generation 7 nm ASIC Deployment

The industry introduced 7 nm miners including the Antminer S17/T17 and WhatsMiner M20S families, delivering around 50–72 TH/s and improved energy efficiency of roughly 40–45 J/TH.


As global Bitcoin network hashrate exceeded 100 EH/s, TerraHash's archive describes procuring a new ASIC fleet and lifting managed computing capacity beyond 100 PH/s.

×

2019 Feed-in Tariff Arbitrage and Electricity Costs

Hong Kong's feed-in tariff program paid approximately HK$4–5 per kWh for qualifying distributed solar generation, higher than typical mining electricity procurement costs.


TerraHash's archived strategy combines exporting rooftop solar electricity to the grid with lower-cost direct or off-grid electricity for computing operations, aiming to stabilize cash flow and reduce blended energy expenditure.

2019 Green Hashrate Hedging and ROI Strategy

The archived project describes a dynamic hedging approach intended to reduce exposure to changing Bitcoin prices and network difficulty.


The model combines relatively stable rooftop-solar tariff revenue with more volatile Bitcoin mining returns. This was presented as a way to support fleet cash flow during price declines; investment outcomes are not guaranteed.
