2016 Strategic Overview in Hong Kong

In 2016, following the ratification of the Paris Agreement, Hong Kong established the inter-departmental Steering Committee on Climate Change. TerraHash Energy expanded its core solutions into large-scale district energy management, real-time demand-side response, and next-generation IoT integration across Hong Kong’s major infrastructure developments.

2016 Hong Kong Regional Development Blueprint

Post-Paris Policy & Utility Reforms

  • Steering Committee Established: Chief Secretary led new climate action steering framework.
  • Scheme of Control (SCA): Negotiations kicked off for post-2018 feed-in tariff and RE integration.

Kai Tak District Cooling System (DCS)

  • Infrastructure Phase II: Expanded seawater-cooled central air-conditioning network.
  • Smart Optimization: Real-time load allocation for Kai Tak Cruise Terminal & hospitals.

Kowloon East Smart City Pilot Zone

  • EKEO Low-Carbon Trial: Smart energy sensors deployed in Kwun Tong industrial revamping.
  • Microgrid Management: Automated energy balance systems for commercial towers.

2016 Paris Agreement Response and Utility Regulation

Hong Kong formed a Steering Committee on Climate Change in 2016, led by the Chief Secretary, to coordinate its response to the Paris Agreement.


The government opened discussions with CLP Power and HK Electric on new Scheme of Control Agreements, including future feed-in tariffs and allowable returns. TerraHash's archived plan describes preparing standardized smart-meter and distributed-energy interfaces.

×

2016 Kai Tak District Cooling System

Kai Tak's district cooling system is a major public infrastructure initiative that uses centralized seawater cooling to serve buildings across the development area.


During its second-stage expansion in 2016, the archived project describes predictive water-flow and temperature-differential controls for pump operation, responding to demand from the cruise terminal and nearby facilities, with a claimed cooling-energy saving of 35%.

2016 Kowloon East Smart-City Pilot

The Energizing Kowloon East Office promoted a Smart Kowloon East program to transform older industrial districts around Kwun Tong and Kowloon Bay into more efficient commercial areas.


TerraHash's archived work describes edge-computing energy monitors and microgrid controls designed to automate load shifting for air-conditioning and lighting in revitalized industrial buildings and offices.

2016 West Kowloon Grid and Substation Telemetry

Expansion of the West Kowloon Cultural District and high-speed rail construction increased electricity demand in the area during 2016.


The archived collaboration with CLP Power describes IoT telemetry at high-voltage substations, providing millisecond-level transformer-temperature and instantaneous-load monitoring to support grid reliability.
