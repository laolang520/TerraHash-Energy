2014 Strategic Overview in Hong Kong

In 2014, TerraHash Energy transitioned from foundational engineering to full-scale commercial pilot deployment across Hong Kong. Building upon our 2013 APAC HQ setup in Central, TerraHash accelerated its integration of intelligent energy storage management (ESS) and smart microgrid control platforms tailored for high-density urban infrastructure and thermal power assets.

Amid the HKSAR Government’s landmark public consultation on Future Fuel Mix, TerraHash played a pivotal role in optimizing regional auxiliary power efficiency, enabling utility partners to transition toward cleaner natural gas imports and distributed renewables with enhanced grid resilience.

2014 Hong Kong Clean Energy Ecosystem

Future Fuel Mix & Policy Reforms

  • Public Consultation Launch: Environment Bureau officially launched the Public Consultation on Future Fuel Mix for Electricity Generation in Hong Kong.
  • Grid Expansion Options: Proposed increasing local natural gas generation to 60% alongside exploring cross-border clean energy grid imports.
Environment & Ecology Bureau ↗

Floating Solar & Reservoir Power

  • Reservoir Solar Feasibility: Water Supplies Department (WSD) initiated technical studies for floating PV systems across local reservoirs like Shek Pik and Plover Cove.
  • Waste-to-Energy Infrastructure: Statutory approvals progressed for the Integrated Waste Management Facility (I·PARK 1) to generate energy from waste.
Water Supplies Department ↗

Commercial Microgrids & Smart Storage

  • Grid Telemetry Expansion: CLP Power Hong Kong enhanced energy storage pilot trials and smart grid monitoring across major power generation stations.
  • Buildings Efficiency Code: EMSD enforced Phase II Building Energy Code updates, incentivizing smart load management in Kowloon East commercial towers.

2014 TerraHash Energy HK Development Timeline

March 2014

Launch of Pilot Smart Energy Storage Deployment (Black Point, Tuen Mun)

TerraHash Energy partnered with industrial engineering contractors at Black Point Power Station in Tuen Mun to deploy a 2MW/4MWh containerized battery energy storage pilot (BESS). The system mitigates peak auxiliary power swings during gas-turbine ramp-ups.

Industry Milestone: Government kicked off the Public Consultation on Future Fuel Mix for Electricity Generation in Hong Kong. Environment Bureau ↗
June 2014

Commercial Microgrid Pilot Demo at Science Park Phase 3 (Pak Shek Kok, Sha Tin)

Coinciding with the grand opening of HKSTP Science Park Phase 3, TerraHash integrated its Microgrid Controller Framework into the building's green automation systems, achieving real-time rooftop solar-wind load balancing.

Industry Milestone: HKSTP officially opened Science Park Phase 3, showcasing high-standard green building and energy-saving designs. HKSTP Official ↗
September 2014

Industrial Waste Heat Recycling Pilot (Castle Peak Power Station, Tap Shek Kok)

Expanded circular thermal energy deployment to Tap Shek Kok, Tuen Mun. TerraHash commissioned a specialized high-efficiency organic Rankine cycle (ORC) recovery unit at Castle Peak Power Station, converting low-grade flue heat directly back into local grid supply.

Industry Milestone: EMSD published updated Energy Efficiency Guidelines for Industrial & Utility Thermal Plants. EMSD Official ↗
December 2014

Green Energy Financing & Regional Expansion (Two IFC, Central)

Headquartered at Two International Finance Centre (Two IFC) in Central, TerraHash Energy secured $25M USD in Series B green growth capital. The funding accelerates deployment of smart energy management platforms across Hong Kong and Greater Bay Area cross-border industrial parks.

Industry Milestone: HKMA and HKEX launched green bond framework discussions to establish HK as an Asia-Pacific Green Finance Hub. HKEX Official ↗
