2024 Strategic Overview: Post-Halving Heavy Power Infrastructure Breakthrough

Following the April 2024 Bitcoin Halving, TerraHash Energy spearheaded a major infrastructure upgrade across its primary construction sites in Texas (USA), North Dakota (USA), Hong Kong (HQ), and Iceland. To maximize efficiency, TerraHash executed a 345kV/230kV Substation Modernization Program, commissioning 500MVA oil-immersed transformers and Gas-Insulated Switchgear (GIS) directly adjacent to major wind energy corridors.
